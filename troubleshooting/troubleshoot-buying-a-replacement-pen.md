# Troubleshoot buying a replacement pen

## Overview

You must be very careful in buying a replacement pen. The best answer is to <mark style="color:red;">**buy the exact same model pen that came with your tablet**</mark>.

## Compatible pens

For a detailed breakdown of the compatibility topic,  see this: [**Pen compatibility with drawing tablets**](../guides/pens/pen-compatibility-with-drawing-tablets.md)

## Always verify compatibility before you buy a replacement pen

No matter what is written on any site, I **HIGHLY ENCOURAGE** you to <mark style="color:red;">**always contact customer support**</mark> for your tablet and ask them to verify which pen models are compatible.
