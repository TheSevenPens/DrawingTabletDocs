# When drawing tablets go on sale

## The short answer

If you can wait, **November is the first sales window I'd watch**. It had the broadest selection of offers in the U.S. sale history I looked at. But I wouldn't wait for Black Friday just because of the name. The same tablet can hit the same price earlier in the year, or go lower.

For a new pen tablet or pen display, **about 20% off is a normal sale** in the offers I checked. Around **5–10%** is a small discount. Around **30% or more** is a strong discount **if the price is really that much lower than what the exact model usually sells for**.

This guide uses past U.S. offers in USD. The examples aren't current price quotes, and timing, stock and prices vary by country and seller.

## When to look

| When | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Late June through July** | Amazon's midyear Prime Day and manufacturer promotions. Prime Day was in July in 2023–2025, but moved to June in 2026. Check the actual dates each year. |
| **August and September** | XPPen's anniversary sales and Huion's back-to-school deals. Check the brand stores as well as retailers. |
| **Early October** | Check for fall offers, including Amazon Prime Big Deal Days. Wacom, Huion and XPPen all had examples in the sample. Some offers require a membership. |
| **November into December** | Check in early November and again around Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Some brand-store sales start weeks before Black Friday or run into December. The event dates don't tell you how long an individual deal will last. |

Spring sales and other promotions happen too. I wouldn't count on any one model going on sale at a particular time.

## Do the brands actually run sales?

* **Wacom: YES.** Check the [Wacom U.S. sale page](https://estore.wacom.com/en-us/sales.html) and compare the same model at retailers. Wacom's own store advertised $80 off the current-generation Intuos Pro Medium and $500 off the Cintiq Pro 17 in November 2026. Wacom deals aren't limited to Black Friday: the same Intuos Pro Medium price appeared in June, late September, early October and November 2026.
* **Huion: YES.** Look at its own store and Amazon. The sample includes midyear Prime Day, [back-to-school offers](https://www.huion.com/news/huion-2026-back-to-school-gear-guide), and Black Friday. Check whether the price requires clipping a coupon.
* **XPPen: YES.** Check the brand store around its [August anniversary](https://www.xp-pen.com/19th-anniversary-celebration.html), not just November. XPPen's store and Amazon can have different dates and prices.
* **Xencelabs: YES.** Its [archived 2025 Black Friday newsletter](https://milled.com/xencelabs/black-friday-2025-is-here-oLhzVWtDYW4pY9do) lists select offers. Xencelabs is outside the price sample below.

Wacom's [education offers](https://estore.wacom.com/en-us/education-exclusive-offers.html) have eligibility requirements. Its [certified refurbished products](https://estore.wacom.com/en-us/specials/special-offers-refurbished.html) aren't new. Compare them separately from a public sale on a new tablet, and check warranty and returns.

In the [historical sample](https://github.com/TheSevenPens/DrawTabResearch/tree/main/investigations/drawing-tablet-sales-us) of 460 comparable advertised offers, the middle half fell between **16.0% and 26.3% off**. The median was 20.1% for Black Friday/Cyber offers and 20.0% outside that window. The median was about 20% for each of Wacom, Huion and XPPen. The sample covers pen tablets and pen displays, not standalone tablets. It was not a comparison of the same models across all sale windows. For example, the Kamvas Pro 19 above was listed at **$799** in [November 2026](https://www.parkablogs.com/content/huion-black-friday-2026-sale), $52 more than in June.

## How I would shop

1. Pick the exact [model number](../guides/general/model-names-vs-model-numbers.md), size and bundle you want. The 2025 Intuos Pro Medium (PTK-670) and the older PTH-660 have very similar names.
2. Check the price from more than one seller and look at recent prices for that same model. Set a price you'd be happy to pay. A large percentage off a crossed-out price doesn't tell you how much you're saving over last week's price.
3. Compare the total at checkout. Check shipping, coupons, membership, included accessories, condition, warranty and return policy. If you're already at your target, you don't have to wait for another sale.
