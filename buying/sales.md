# Drawing tablets sales

## Caveats

* The is based on looking at historical sales in the US market. I'm not able to talk about sales in other regions or how discounts work.
* It does not include pre-owned or refurbished tablets.

## Best chance for a discount

**November is the first sales window to watch**. It generally has broadest selection of offers in the U.S. sale history I looked at.&#x20;

**Should you wait for "Black Friday" sales in November?** No. The same tablet can hit the same price earlier in the year, or go lower.

## How much of a discount?

* The median discount is 20%. median means half of the discounts are less than or equal to this value and half are greater than this value
* Smaller discounts range from 5 to 10%.
* A really good discount is 30% or more

## Sales and opportunities

| When                       | What to look for                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Late June through July** | Amazon's midyear Prime Day and manufacturer promotions. Prime Day was in July in 2023–2025, but moved to June in 2026. Check the actual dates each year.                                                                   |
| **August and September**   | XPPen's anniversary sales and Huion's back-to-school deals. Check the brand stores as well as retailers.                                                                                                                   |
| **Early October**          | Check for fall offers, including Amazon Prime Big Deal Days. Wacom, Huion and XPPen all had examples in the sample. Some offers require a membership.                                                                      |
| **November into December** | Check in early November and again around Black Friday and Cyber Monday. Some brand-store sales start weeks before Black Friday or run into December. The event dates don't tell you how long an individual deal will last. |
| **Other**                  | There are also sales in the spring and various other promotions.                                                                                                                                                           |

## Brand-run sales

The brands themselves run sales.

* **Wacom:** Check the [Wacom U.S. sale page](https://estore.wacom.com/en-us/sales.html) and compare the same model at retailers.&#x20;
* **Huion:** Look at its own store and Amazon. You might find sales during midyear Prime Day, [back-to-school offers](https://www.huion.com/news/huion-2026-back-to-school-gear-guide), and Black Friday. Check whether the price requires clipping a coupon.
* **XPPen:** Check the brand store around its [August anniversary](https://www.xp-pen.com/19th-anniversary-celebration.html), not just November. XPPen's store and Amazon can have different dates and prices.

Wacom's [education offers](https://estore.wacom.com/en-us/education-exclusive-offers.html) have eligibility requirements. Its [certified refurbished products](https://estore.wacom.com/en-us/specials/special-offers-refurbished.html) aren't new. Compare them separately from a public sale on a new tablet, and check warranty and returns.
