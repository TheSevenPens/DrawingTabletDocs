# Beginner standalone tablets

## If I had to pick one

**Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE** ([<mark style="background-color:green;">**My notes**</mark>](../../drawing-tablets-links/samsung/samsung-galaxy-tab-s-series/samsung-galaxy-tab-s.md) ) - this has a decent drawing experience, and also works well as an iPad-like device.

## Other good options

* **Apple iPad** - Any Apple iPad that works with the Apple Pencil 2 ([<mark style="background-color:green;">**My notes**</mark>](../../drawing-tablets-links/apple/apple-ipad-1.md))&#x20;
  * You will have the buy the Apple Pencil 2 separately. It does NOT come with the iPad.
* **Wacom MovinkPad 11**
  * **The best drawing experience.**
  * Pen comes with it.
  * Don't lose the pen - it is $130 to purchase a replacement.
* **Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE** ([<mark style="background-color:green;">**My notes**</mark>](../../drawing-tablets-links/samsung/samsung-galaxy-tab-s-series/samsung-galaxy-tab-s.md) )
  * More info: [**7P notes: Apple iPad versus Samsung Galaxy Tab S**](../../drawing-tablets-links/apple/apple-ipad-versus-samsung-galaxy-tab-s.md)&#x20;
  * The Samsung S Pen COMES WITH the tablet.
  * If you are one a smaller budget, Samsung Galaxy Tab S6 lite (2022 or 2024 edition)  ([<mark style="background-color:green;">**My notes**</mark>](../../drawing-tablets-links/samsung/samsung-galaxy-tab-s-series/samsung-galaxy-tab-s.md)) - a little bit sluggish but the price is great. The Samsun S pen also comes with this tablet.
