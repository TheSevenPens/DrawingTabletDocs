# Recommended drawing tablets for beginners

## Overview

These are highly-curated recommendations for people who are just beginning to get into drawing tablets.&#x20;

* All these tablets deliver the basic experiences and a reasonable price
* Some of these tablets are missing so-called "pro" features.

These are tablets WITHOUT a screen that need to be connected to a computer (some support wireless) to work.

## Standalone tablets

These are tablets that don't need to be connect to a computer.

More info: [**7P notes: Apple iPad versus Samsung Galaxy Tab S**](../drawing-tablets-links/apple/apple-ipad-versus-samsung-galaxy-tab-s.md)&#x20;

* **Apple iPad** - Any Apple iPad that works with the Apple Pencil 2 ([<mark style="background-color:green;">**My notes**</mark>](../drawing-tablets-links/apple/apple-ipad-1.md))&#x20;
  * NOTE: You will have the buy the Apple Pencil 2 separately. It does NOT come with the iPad.
* **Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 FE** ([<mark style="background-color:green;">**My notes**</mark>](../drawing-tablets-links/samsung/samsung-galaxy-tab-s.md) )
  * NOTE: The Samsung S Pen COMES WITH the tablet.
  * If you are one a smaller budget, Samsung Galaxy Tab S6 lite (2022 or 2024 edition)  ([<mark style="background-color:green;">**My notes**</mark>](../drawing-tablets-links/samsung/samsung-galaxy-tab-s.md)) - a little bit sluggish but the price is great. The Samsun S pen also comes with this tablet.
