# Upgrading from Huion PW517 to Huion PW550 pens

## Overview

If your tablet came with the PW517 pen, consider upgrading to the PW550 or PW550S pens.

The PW550 series pens are backwards compatible with tablets that use the PW517 pen, and these PW500 pens offer superior pressure handling.

The video below explains the improvements and the potential for upgrading in great detail.
