# Huion PW100 pen notes

## Basics

Store link: [https://store.huion.com/products/battery-free-pen-pw100](https://store.huion.com/products/battery-free-pen-pw100) ([archive](https://archive.is/4Hxx7))

## Compatibility

Huion lists these as compatible tablets: H640P, H950P, H1060P, H1161, H610 Pro V2, HS64, HS610, Inspiroy Ink H320M&#x20;

## Pressure range

I only have one unit and based on my testing

* The IAF is about 12gf
* The maximum pressure is about 250gf
