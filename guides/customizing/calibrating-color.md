# Calibrating color

## Overview

For other calibration types, see [Calibrating a drawing tablet](calibrating.md).

**Formal color calibration** is a specific process. You measure and adjust a display's output. Its colors, brightness, and contrast then match industry standards. These include sRGB, Adobe RGB, and DCI-P3. This requires display support, specialized calibration hardware, software, and expertise.

**Informal color calibration** includes adjustments to color, brightness, and contrast. These adjustments suit your preferences or better match another display. They do not require calibration hardware.
