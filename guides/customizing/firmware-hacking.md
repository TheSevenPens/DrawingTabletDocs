# Firmware hacking

## Overview

WARNING: I DO NOT RECOMMEND TRYING TO MODIFY THE FIRMWARE OF YOUR TABLET.

Don't do it.

## Timeline

For a very long time, some people have wanted to modify the firmware of tablets to modify their behavior. And for a long time attempts to modify the firmware have ended up in bricked tablets.

In Septer\mber 2026 we saw significant developments in this area that have made firmware modifications possible and vastly reduced the chances of bricking the device.

## Why are people interested in modifying the firmware?

Primarily the audience is from the osu community. To play play osu they have wanted much higher tablet report rates.&#x20;

## Current status

Currently there are several groups working on:

* modifying tablet firmware
* potentially even writing entirely new custom firmware

Several thousand people have successfully modified their firmware to achieve higher report rates.&#x20;

For artists, currently the changes may improve the report rate but pressure does not work as well. So for now, we will have to be content with the stock firmware in our tablets.
