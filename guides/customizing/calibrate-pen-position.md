# Calibrating pen position

## Overview

On a pen display, you may point at one location with the pen, but the pointer appears somewhere else. This could be due to the pen being incorrectly calibrated to the display.

If you see the word "Calibrate" or "Calibration" anywhere in a tablet driver UI - it almost always is referring to Calibration pen position/

For other kinds of calibration:, see [Calibrating a drawing tablet](calibrating-color.md)

## What about pen displays?

Most pen displays offer a pen calibration to solve this problem.

Some, like the Wacom Cintiq Pro 27, do not offer this calibration because they are well-calibrated when you get them.

## What about pen tablets?

Pen calibration is a feature found in pen displays. Pen tablets don't need the feature because they don't have screens

## Availability

* Calibrating pen position is offered on most pen displays
* Calibrating pen position is NOT offered on pen tablets
