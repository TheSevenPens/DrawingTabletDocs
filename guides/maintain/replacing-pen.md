# Replacing a lost or damaged pen

## Overview

If you need a replacement pen for your tablet, you cannot buy just any pen, even from the same manufacturer.

## Selecting the right pen

You have to be careful when selecting a pen. Most tablets are only compatible with a small number of pens, and sometimes only with a single pen. To keep it simple: <mark style="color:red;">**buy the exact same model pen that came with your tablet**</mark>.

Many people accidentally buy incompatible pens and get frustrated. Avoid this by doing a little research. For a detailed breakdown of compatibility, see [Pen compatibility](../pens/pen-compatibility.md).

## Instructions

* First, find the model number of your tablet: [Finding the model number of your drawing tablet](../general/finding-tablet-model-number.md)
* Then go to your tablet brand's website and search for your tablet. It should list the compatible pen model number. If you cannot find what you are looking for, **contact customer support**.
* If you have a **Wacom** tablet, look at this compatibility list: [Wacom pen compatibility](../../catalog/pens/wacom-pens/wacom-pen-compatibility.md)

## ALWAYS verify with customer support

No matter what is written on any site, I **highly encourage** you to <mark style="color:red;">**always contact customer support**</mark> for your tablet and ask them to verify which pen models are compatible.
