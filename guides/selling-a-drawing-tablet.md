# Selling a drawing tablet

## Generally, consider the perspective of the buyer

These docs may help you:

* [Buying used drawing tablets](../buying-a-drawing-tablet/buying-used-drawing-tablets.md)
* [Inspecting a drawing tablet](../buying-a-drawing-tablet/inspecting-a-drawing-tablet.md)

## Where to sell

* eBay
* Vinted

## How to price

* Search on seller locations and see what they are generally  being sold for.

## Selling old tablets

* For practical reasons, most people want relatively new tablets&#x20;
* Buyers may have to do special things to get older tablets to work

## Tips

* Take pictures in BRIGHT LIGHTING so that the tablet can be identified
* Take a picture of the MODEL NUMBER of the tablet. It's usually on the back.
* Add the MODEL NUMBER to the listing

## Prepaing the tablet

* Thoroughly CLEAN the surfaces tablets and pens.
