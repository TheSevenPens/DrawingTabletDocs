# Microsoft Surface Pro 7 notes

## Summary

I don't normally recommend the Surface Pro 7 because its drawing experience is not great. For some people, it might work well enough.

For example, it might serve well enough as a general laptop with occasional pen use, for note-taking, marking up documents, and whiteboarding. But for creative tasks, it is not what I recommend.

## Details

The pen experience on Surface Pro 7 is not great. It can exhibit some noticeable diagonal wobble. The experience got much better with the Surface Pro 8 and the newer Surface Slim Pen 2, but it is still not as good as a normal drawing tablet.

The SP7 might be enough for your needs if it has enough RAM and storage, and it might work well enough for creative tasks like drawing.

If you are interested in an SP7 try to use one for a little bit before you purchase.
