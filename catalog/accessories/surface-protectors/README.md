# Surface protection

## Overview

There are many options to protect the surface of your tablet from scratches, etc. There are options for both pen tablets (screenless tablets) and pen displays (screen tablets). There are options that are specific to devices such as iPads

* [Surface protectors](surface-protectors/)
* [Screen protectors](screen-protectors/)
* [Screen protectors for iPads](screen-protectors/screen-protectors-for-ipads.md)

## Protectors do not interfere with the pen

These protective sheets do NOT interfere with how your pen works. The EMR signal from the pen, passes right through non-metallic materials such as plastic or glass.
