# Kala Paint

{% hint style="warning" %}
Kala Paint is in **alpha** (version 0.1.34a as of November 2026). It is available to buy and has a free trial, but features are still being added and polished.
{% endhint %}

## Overview

Kala Paint is a desktop painting app made by Oliver Lawson and published by Cooldog Studios Ltd.&#x20;

Product site: [https://kalapaint.app/](https://kalapaint.app/)

## Status and platforms

As listed on the product site in November 2026:

* **Windows** 10 and 11 - alpha
* **Linux** - alpha
* **Android** - planned
* **iPad** - planned.
* macOS is not listed.

## Pricing & Licensing

* One time purchase: YES - $79.99 USD
* Subscription: NO. The creator says it will never become subscription-based.
* Multiple machines - YES - A license includes updates for the current major version and comes with 3 activations that can be moved between any mix of Windows and Linux machines.
* Trail available: YES - 14 days
