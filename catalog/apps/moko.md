# Moko

{% hint style="warning" %}
Moko is in **closed beta** (as of November 2026). It is available only to invited testers, and features may change before release.
{% endhint %}

## Overview

Moko is a desktop app for digital painting and image editing from Cubebrush. According to the description of the review video below, it is designed by Marc Brunet and his development team.

Product site: [https://moko.art/](https://moko.art/)

## Status and platforms

As listed on the product site in November 2026:

* **Windows** and **macOS** - closed beta, available to invited testers.
* **iPad** - a tablet version is on the roadmap.
* **Linux** - on the roadmap.
* No release dates have been announced. Requesting beta access does not guarantee a place.

## Pricing

The product site says it will be a one-time purchase with a perpetual license and no subscriptions planned. No price has been announced.

## Drawing tablet and pen support

The product site lists "Pressure & tilt" among its features. It does not name specific tablets, pens, or drivers, and tablet and pen behavior has not been tested for this page yet.

## Features

Features the product site says are already in the beta:

* Brushes: natural paints with pigment mixing, dual and multi-tip brushes, a live brush editor, and brushes made from your artwork
* Layers, masks, clipping masks, blend modes, gradient maps, adjustment layers, and layer styles
* Selection, bucket and lasso fill, and transform tools
* Symmetry painting, reference boards, timelapse recording, text, color harmonies, and vector shapes
* Customizable workspace with floating panels
* Import of Photoshop ABR brushes, Clip Studio Paint SUT/SUTG brushes, and layered PSD files

A photography / RAW workspace is listed as in development. The Natural Paint feature needs a GPU with WebGPU support.
