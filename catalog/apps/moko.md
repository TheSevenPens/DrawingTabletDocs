# Moko

{% hint style="warning" %}
Moko is in **closed beta** (as of November 2026). It is available only to invited testers, and features may change before release.
{% endhint %}

## Overview

Moko is a desktop app for digital painting and image editing from Marc Brunet/Cubebrush. .

Product site: [https://moko.art/](https://moko.art/)

{% hint style="info" %}
Be aware, "Moko" seems to be a popular name for apps. There are many apps that share the same name. Be careful when you search for it. The official site is [https://moko.art/](https://moko.art/)
{% endhint %}

## Status and&#x20;

As of November 2026:

* **Windows** and **macOS** - closed beta, available to invited testers.
* **iPad** - on the roadmap.
* **Linux** - on the roadmap.

## Licensing & pricing

* The product site says it will be a one-time purchase with a perpetual license and no subscriptions planned.&#x20;
* No specific price has been announced.
