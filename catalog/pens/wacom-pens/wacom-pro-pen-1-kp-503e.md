# Wacom Pro Pen 1 (KP-503E)

## Overview

The KP-503E was the first with the name "Pro Pen" in Wacom's KP series.

* Pressure levels: 2048
* Tilt: YES
* Barrel rotation: NO
* Buttons: 2
