# Wacom Pro Pen 3 (ACP-500) vs Wacom Pro Pen 2 (KP-504E)

## Overview

This document summarizes the differences between these ACP-500 and the KP-504E.

For more details:

* [Wacom Pro Pen 3 (ACP-500) notes](wacom-acp500-notes.md)
* [Wacom Pro Pen 2 (KP-504E) notes](wacom-kp504e-notes.md)&#x20;

## Summary

The Pro Pen 3 is similar in drawing performance to the Pro Pen 2- with one notablet difference. The ACP-500 seems to have a slightly higher IAF than the Pro Pen 2.

Overall, opinions seems to be vary polarized. Some people strongly prefer the ACP-500 while many continue to feel that the KP-504E is clearly superior.

## Key differences

### Modularity

WINNER: ACP-500

ACP-500 offers customizability in terms of weight, shape, grips. KP-504E has a single wight, shape, and grip.

### Ergonomics

WINNER: UNCLEAR

Personally I like the KP-504E in terms of how it feels in the hand. Others strongly prefer the ACP-500.

### Pressure Levels

WINNER: TIED

Both support 8192 pressure levels

### IAF

WINNER: KP-504E

KP-504E tends to have a lower IAF than ACP-500.

### MAX Physical Pressure

WINNER: KP-504E

Both ACP-500 and KP-504E have maximum pressures in the excellent range. However, KP-504 has n even higher maximum pressue on average.

### Number of buttons

WINNER: ACP-500

* ACP-500 has 3 buttons
* KP-504E has 2 buttons

### Eraser

WINNER: KP-504E

* ACP-500 does NOT have an eraser
* KP-504E has an eraser
