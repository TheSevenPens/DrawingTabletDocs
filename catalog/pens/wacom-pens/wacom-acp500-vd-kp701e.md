# Wacom Art Pen 2 (ACP-500) vs Art Pen 1 (KP-701)

## Summary

Both the Art Pen 2 and Art Pen 1 are fantastic pens in terms of performance. In addition the Art Pen 2 improves upon the formula with a more ergonomic design and 3 buttons - but at the same time the Art Pen 2 loses an eraser and doesn't have quite the low IAF I found in the Art Pen 1. The Art Pen 2 is also compatible with far fewer tablets.

Note the Art Pen 2 is also referred to as the Intuos 4 Art Pen because it was launched along with the Wacom Intuos 4 tablet series.

## IAF

WINNER: Art Pen 1 has reliably lower IAF in my testing where I compare 3 units of each model.

* The Art Pen 1 has a IAF of \~ 1.5gf
* The Art Pen 2 has a IAF of a \~3.4gf

## MAX Pressure

WINNER: Art Pen 2 has a higher maximum pressure but the Art Pen 1's MAX pressure is still good.

* The Art Pen 1 has a IAF of \~ 370gf
* The Art Pen 2 has a max pressure of a \~575gf

## Ergonomics

WINNER: Art Pen 2

I found The Art Pen 2 easier to hold. I the more narrow tip worked better for more angled strokes. With the Art Pen 1 the tip of the pen is thick enough it can contact the tablet surface even before you achieve maximum tilt

## Eraser

WINNER: Art Pen 1

Only the Art Pen 1 has an eraser. Wacom removed he eraser from the Art Pen 2.

## Buttons

WINNER: Art Pen 2

The Art Pen 1 has to buttons and the Art Pen 2 has 3 buttons.

I found the buttons on the Art Pen 2 to be better and less prone to accidental clicking.

## Pointer lag

NO DIFFERENCE - this is expected. Pens do not influence how much pointer lag is experienced.

## Look & Feel

WINNER: Art Pen 2

Easier to hold because of its shape. The slightly rubberized texture feels nice.

## Tablet compatibility

WINNER: Art Pen 1

The Art Pen 1 is compatible with about 30 Wacom tablets. The Art Pen 2 is compatible with about 7 in July of 2026 - that number is expected to increase to 9 by about the end of 2026.
