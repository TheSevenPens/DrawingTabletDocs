# Wacom stroke nib

## Overview

The Wacom stroke nib is a very unusual nib with a spring in the middle.

Cost - This is considered a premium nib, and is relatively expensive at $2 USD per nib. So the standard pack of 5 stroke nibs costs $10 USD.

Effect - The nib does not affect pressure (IAF, etc.), but some people find it very pleasant to draw with.
