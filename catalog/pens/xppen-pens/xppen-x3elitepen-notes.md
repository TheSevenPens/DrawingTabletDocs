# XP-Pen X3 Elite pen notes

## Overview

Pretty good - surprisingly good for a consumer level tablet. IAF could be a little better but not a big problem for me.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image-000753.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Buttons

Two buttons on pen. It looks like one button - but it is a rocker switch so acts like two buttons.

## Eraser

No eraser.

## IAF

I found the IAF to vary from as low as 3.7gf to 9.8gf with a median of 5.3gf. That 9.8gf measurement may be an outlier.

| Statistic | IAF (gf) |
| --------- | -------- |
| Min       | 3.7      |
| Median    | 5.3      |
| Max       | 9.8      |
