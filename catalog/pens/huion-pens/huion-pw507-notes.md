# Huion PW507 pen notes

Store link: [https://store.huion.com/products/battery-free-pen-pw507](https://store.huion.com/products/battery-free-pen-pw507)

## Compatible tablets

Huion lists these tablets as compatible:

* GT-191 V2 (700K)
* Kamvas Pro 12
* Kamvas Pro 13
* Kamvas Pro 16
* Kamvas 16
* Pro 20 (2019)
* Kamvas 20

Pen compatibility as of 2026-02-06 from Huion's website.
