## Models

## Core specs

* Pressure levels: 2048
* Digitizer resolution: 5080 LPI (200 LPMM)
* Tilt range: ±60°

## Included pen

Grip pen (KP-501E)

## Pen compatibility

* Wacom Grip pen (KP-501E)
* Wacom Pro Pen (KP-503E)
* Wacom Art Pen (KP-701E)
* Wacom Classic Pen (KP-300E)
* Wacom Airbrush Pen (KP-400E)
