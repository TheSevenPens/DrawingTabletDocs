# Wacom Intuos 2026 (CTK-x120) notes

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Unreleased.** Wacom announced these tablets and opened pre-orders on 2026-09-29, but they have not shipped yet. Everything below comes from Wacom's published specs, not from hands-on testing.
{% endhint %}

## Overview

The Intuos 2026 is the successor to the [Wacom Intuos (CTL-x100)](wacom-ctlx100-notes.md). Based on the information we know so far, this greatly improves the Intuos series.

There are two models:

* Intuos 2026 Small (CTK-4120)
* Intuos 2026 Medium (CTK-6120)

## Key changes from the CTL-x100 series

* Supports the new Lite Pen (ACP-502)
* Supports tilt
* 8192 pressure levels
* 300 Hz report rate
* a 16:9 active area
* USB-C port (older models suppored micro-USB)&#x20;

## Notes on specs

### Pens

* Ships with the Wacom Lite Pen (ACP-502).
* According to Wacom it also works with Pro Pen 3 pens and nibs, the Wacom One pen, and Wacom EMR stationery pens.
