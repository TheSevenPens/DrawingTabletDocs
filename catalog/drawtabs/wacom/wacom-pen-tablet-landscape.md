# Wacom pen tablet landscape

## Overview

Wacom has several separate lines of pen tablets (drawing tablets without a screen). This document will help you understand the differences and make your choice heading into 2026 and 2027. There are two tiers for Wacom's pen tablets: Professional and Consumer.

## Quick recommendations

* **Buying your first professional pen tablet** → Intuos Pro 2025 Medium (PTK-670).
* **Already own an Intuos Pro 2017** → Do NOT upgrade. Keep using it.
* **Want to spend less** → Intuos Medium (CTL-6100 or CTL-6100WL), or a One by Wacom Medium (CTL-672) if you can find one at a reasonable price.
* **Do not buy for drawing** → Wacom One 2023 pen tablets (CTC-4110WL, CTC-6110WL).
* **Size** → Medium is the sweet spot for most people.

## Choosing a size

More information: [Choosing the right size for a drawing tablet](../../../buying/choosing-size.md).

* **Small** - Fine for photo editing, small workspaces, or small hands. For drawing it feels cramped and can lead to hand fatigue.
* **Medium (recommended)** - About 10 to 12 inches diagonal active area. This is the sweet spot for the vast majority of artists.
* **Large** - My personal favorite, but you need a desk setup that can fit it, and it suits people who draw with their whole arm.

## Choosing a Wacom professional pen tablet

There are two choices:

* **Intuos Pro 2025** (PTK-470, PTK-670, PTK-870) -> [Wacom Intuos Pro 2025 (PTK-x70) notes](wacom-intuos-pro-2025/wacom-ptkx70-notes.md)
* **Intuos Pro 2017** (PTH-460, PTH-660, PTH-860) -> [Wacom Intuos Pro 2017 (PTH-x60) notes](wacom-intuos-pro-2017/wacom-pthx60-notes.md)

If you want a pen tablet for drawing/sketching/painting and want to go with the "safe choice" then you should pick a Wacom tablet. In particular the Wacom Intuos Pro 2025 and 2017 models are THE BEST PEN TABLETS EVER MADE. The drawing experience of the two is essentially the same.

For a full side-by-side, see [Wacom Intuos Pro 2025 vs Intuos Pro 2017](wacom-intuos-pro-2025-vs-2017.md).

### Pen feel: Pro Pen 2 vs Pro Pen 3

The Pro Pen 2 (KP-504E) that comes with the 2017 edition has, in my opinion, a slightly better drawing feel than the Pro Pen 3 (ACP-500) that comes with the 2025 edition. The difference is small. The Pro Pen 2 also works on the 2025 edition, so you can use it there if you prefer it. More here: [Wacom Pro Pen 3 (ACP-500) vs Wacom Pro Pen 2 (KP-504E)](../../pens/wacom-pens/wacom-acp500-vs-kp504e.md).

### Touch

The 2017 edition supports multitouch. The 2025 edition does not. If you rely on touch gestures, that alone may decide it for you.

### Driver support lifetime

Wacom typically supports a tablet with drivers for about 10 years. Official driver support for the Intuos Pro 2017 is expected to start winding down around 2027. The Intuos Pro 2025 has roughly 9 years of driver support left.

When Wacom drivers stop supporting a tablet, the hardware still works. [OpenTabletDriver](https://docs.sevenpens.com/opentabletdriver/otd-windows-install) is an alternative that can keep older hardware running, although it takes some technical expertise to set up for artistic use.

### Footprint and active area

The 2025 edition is physically smaller and lighter, which saves desk space, while its active area is slightly larger than the 2017 edition. For the Medium size:

* Intuos Pro 2017 Medium (PTH-660): 338 x 219 mm, 700 g, active area 224 x 148 mm
* Intuos Pro 2025 Medium (PTK-670): 291 x 206 mm, 411 g, active area 263 x 148 mm

### Barrel rotation and the Art Pens

Both editions support barrel rotation, but neither included pen does. You need an Art Pen.

* **Intuos Pro 2017** - Needs the original Wacom Art Pen (KP-701E). It is rare now and often sells for around $500 on eBay.
* **Intuos Pro 2025** - Works with the Wacom Art Pen 2 (ACP-700), which costs about $100. This makes the 2025 edition the sensible choice if you want barrel rotation.

More here: [Wacom Art Pen 2 (ACP-700) notes](../../pens/wacom-pens/wacom-acp700-notes.md) and [Wacom Art Pen 2 (ACP-700) vs Art Pen (KP-701E)](../../pens/wacom-pens/wacom-acp700-vs-kp701e.md).

### UD EMR pens

The 2025 edition works with specific pens that use Wacom UD EMR, such as the Samsung S Pen and the Wacom One pen (CP-923). Their stroke quality is not good enough for finished artwork, but they are cheap and make good emergency backup pens. The 2017 edition does not support them. More here: [Compatibility with specific Wacom UD EMR pens](wacom-intuos-pro-2025/wacom-ptkx70-notes.md#compatibility-with-specific-wacom-ud-emr-pens).

### ExpressKeys, bezels and surface texture

* **ExpressKeys** - On the 2017 edition they are on the left side. On the 2025 edition they moved to the top edge.
* **Bezels** - The 2025 edition has narrow bezels that may dig into your wrist at first. The 2017 edition has wide, sloped bezels that are comfortable to rest on.
* **Surface texture** - The 2017 surface is known for eating nibs quickly. The 2025 surface is smoother and softer, feels pleasant, and wears nibs much less.

## Overall drawing experience

All of the tablets except the Wacom One 2023 tablets have a very very good drawing experience. The Intuos Pro series is definitely the best of all of them though - largely driven by the amazing pressure handling of the Wacom Pro Pen 2 and Wacom Pro Pen 3.

## Pen pressure

Learn more here: [Pen pressure](../../../core/pressure/)

**Pressure levels**

2048 **pressure levels** is all you need for creative work. All of these pens are enough in that regard. Wacom has strong marketing towards their higher pressure level tablets, but the vast majority of users will not be able to make use of these higher levels in their work.

More than pressure levels, the **pressure range** has a greater impact on your drawing experience. And this is driven by the quality of the pressure sensor in the pen.

### Notes on pen tilt

TILT - Not all drawing styles require tilt. And if you do want to control the rotation of your brush many drawing apps let you control the brush rotation based on the direction of the pen movement instead of tilt. Learn more here: [Pen tilt](../../../core/tilt/)

## Tablet resolution

Resolution means how many separate points the tablet can distinguish (i.e. resolve) in a given length. This is specified as Lines Per Inch (LPI) though it is also useful to think about it as lines per millimeter (LPMM).

You will not notice the difference between 2540 LPI and 5080 LPI.

* One by Wacom -> 2540 LPI = 100 LPMM
* Wacom One 2023 pen tablets -> 2540 LPI = 100 LPMM
* Intuos -> 2540 LPI = 100 LPMM
* Intuos Pro 2017 -> 5080 LPI = 200 LPMM
* Intuos Pro 2025 -> 5080 LPI = 200 LPMM

## Report rate

* One by Wacom -> 133 Hz
* Intuos -> 133 Hz
* Intuos Pro 2017 -> 200 Hz
* Intuos Pro 2025 -> 300 Hz wired with the Pro Pen 3 (230 Hz over Bluetooth)

More here: [Pen Report Rate](wacom-intuos-pro-2025/wacom-ptkx70-notes.md#pen-report-rate)

## Accuracy

Accuracy = tablet & computer know the correct position of the tip of the pen. As far as I have observed, all of these tablets are very accurate.

## Pointer lag

Pointer lag is the difference between the physical position of the pen and where the operating system pointer is drawn. Pen tablets in general display very little pointer lag. In comparison, pen displays all show very noticeable lag.

* One by Wacom -> very little pointer lag
* Wacom One 2023 pen tablets -> very little pointer lag
* Intuos -> very little lag (when hovering has a little bit of pointer lag)
* Intuos Pro 2017 -> very little pointer lag
* Intuos Pro 2025 -> very little pointer lag

Learn more here: [Pointer lag](../../../core/pointer-lag.md)

## Accuracy / Diagonal wobble

The Intuos Pro models exhibit less wobble than the Intuos or One by Wacom. But all of the tablets are good for diagonal wobble.

## Active area

## Reliability

All of these are very reliable tablets. Their pens are also very reliable. **But remember, DO NOT drop your pens.** They are much more delicate than the tablets and you can break them with a fall.

## Drivers

As of November 2026, the latest Wacom driver works with all of these product lines. Official support for the Intuos Pro 2017 is expected to start winding down around 2027. See [Driver support lifetime](wacom-pen-tablet-landscape.md#driver-support-lifetime) above.

## Surface texture

The Intuos Pro models have a more textured surface. The Intuos and One by Wacom have less texture.

The Intuos Pro 2017 has more texture than the Intuos Pro 2025. That texture wears nibs down quickly. The Intuos Pro 2025 surface is smoother and softer and causes much less nib wear.

## Texture sheets

Wacom sells texture sheets for the Intuos Pro 2017. Three texture options are provided for both the Medium and Large sizes.

Wacom sells texture sheets for the Intuos Pro 2025. One texture option is provided for the Small, Medium and Large sizes.
