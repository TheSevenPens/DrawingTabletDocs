# USI

## Overview

Universal Stylus Initiative: [https://universalstylus.org/](https://universalstylus.org/)&#x20;

The Universal Stylus Initiative is (was?) an organization to create apparently some kind of stylus specification. Ultimately, few styluses are USI and those that are are poor quality compared to a traditional EMR pen. I highly recommend avoiding devices that use a USI stylus.&#x20;
