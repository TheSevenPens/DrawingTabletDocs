# Testing drawing exeperience



## Overview

Here are the overall set of things I look for when evaluating a drawing tablet in relation to the DRAWING EXPERIENCE.

### Expressiveness

There are three "sensors" available to make expressive strokes that respond to what your hand is doing:

* Pressure
* Tilt
* Barrel rotation

Apps map those sensors to different facets of a brush to provide the "feeling" you want. Typically the would be a mapping like this:

* Pressure to brush size or brush opacity
* Tilt to Brush size (usually along one dimention) or Brush rotation
* Barrel - Brush rotation

What I am generally looking for:

* **Smooth pressure transition** - strokes going from low pressure to high pressure without any wierd hiccups.&#x20;
* **Artifacts at low pressure** - EMR pens are generally over-reactive near the IAF. This means drawing at low pressure can produce weird blobby pressure response. This is very common but also correctable with pressure smoothing or curving.
* **Pressure scan rate** - Being good here means that lots of fast dots or quick strokes will result in none of them being missed.

### Positioning

* **Pointer tracking accuracy (vertical & static)** - When holding a pen still and vertical to the surface of the tablet - is the pointer directly under the tip of the pen?
* **Tilt compensation** - this is exactly the same as pointer tracking accuracy except non-vertical but still static. There's always a little drift from the position directly under the pen tip when the pen is tilted. Sometimes this will depend on the specific amount or even direction of the tilt.
* Diagonal wobble&#x20;
* Parallax&#x20;
* Pointer lag&#x20;
