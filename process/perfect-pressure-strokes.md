# Perfect pressure strokes

## Overview

For some kinds of testing or investigation, it's useful to remove "the human element". With pressure, it's not possible for a human to push down on the tib of the pen with a perfect monotonically changing amount of pressure.

To this purpose, instead I use a Kuuube's pressure emulation plugin for OpenTabletDriver.

You can download the plugin from here: [https://github.com/Kuuuube/Pressure\_Emulation](https://github.com/Kuuuube/Pressure_Emulation)&#x20;

## My standard configuration

Pressure Resolution - to simulate N levels of pressure. Shown below is 256. Setting it to zero will not affect the pressure values

Line length - 800

Line X Offset - 200
