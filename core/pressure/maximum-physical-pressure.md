# Maximum physical pressure

## Overview

Maximum physical pressure is the maximum amount of physical force a pen can uniquely detect.

## Relationship to pressure range

The Maximum physical pressure is a the upper-bound of the pen's [pressure range](pen-pressure-range.md) and thus it is the logical opposite of the [IAF](iaf.md).

## What happens when physical pressure is more than the maximum

Let's suppose that for a physical force of 499gf that the pen reports a pressure level of 99.9%.

And then at 500gf, the pen will report 100% pressure. This means 500gf is the maximum physical pressure.

If the physical force is 501gf, then the pen will continue to report 100% pessure. In other words if the pen is reporting 100% pressure you don't know that the pen is exactly at its maximum physical pressure, it could be that value or it could be any value above the maximum physical pressure.

## What is a good maximum physical pressure

Generally, you want a pen with a high maximum physical pressure.

Anything at 350gf and above is good.

Anything at 500gf to 900gf is excellent.

Approaching 900gf makes it hard to get to 100% pressure.&#x20;

## Can maximum physical pressure be too high?

Yes. Any pen with a max physical pressure at 900gf is very hard for someone to press down to get 100% pressure.&#x20;

* Some people simply cannot press down that hard
* Some people can press down that hard, but it is difficult for them or painful
* Some peopel find it very tiring

The good news is, you can artificially reduce maximum physical pressure with a pressure curve. See: [Lowering maximum physical pressure](../../guides/customizing/lowering-max-physical-pressure.md).

## Can maximum physical pressure be too low?

YES.

When a pen has a maximum physical pressure at 200gf or less. It's very easy for a user to even accidentally hit 100% pressure. This means that strokes can sometimes look very blotchy. People might describe it as veyr hard to control pressure.

## Variability in pens

The maximum physical pressure will vary between pen models.

However be aware even untis of the same pen model can vary substantially in their maximum physical pressure.

For example for some pens, the variability might be +/- 50gf - which would be considered very good - a very low amount of variation.

But other pens might ahve a variability of +/- 200gf - which would be considered a lot of variation.

Low variation is desirable. Because it means if you replace a pen with another of the same model, then you can expect the new pen to behave like the old pen in terms of maximum physical pressure.
