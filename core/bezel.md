# Bezel

## Overview

The bezel of a drawing tablet is the area on its surface that is not the [Active area](active-area/).

For many devices such as phones and TVs, people make a big deal about having the smallest bezels possible. For drawing tablets, a wide bezel is often helpful.

Do people like bezels? There is no clear pattern. Some like wider bezels. Some like smaller bezels

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (111).png" alt="" width="563"><figcaption><p>This 24" pen display has a 2" bezel that causes the overall size of the pen display to be close to 30"</p></figcaption></figure>

## Benefits

### Ergonomic benefits

* Avoids your pen uncomfortably "dropping off" when you make strokes near the edge of the active area
* Provides a place for you to rest your wrist or forearm so the tablet edge does not dig into them.

### Drawing experience

The EMR sensor (digitizer) needs to be a bit wider than the active area so that it is still accurate at the edges. A wider bezel provides that extra space for the digitizer. And thus there is a little more accuracy at the edges of the active area.

## Drawbacks

Large bezels result in tablets that weigh more and take up more space compared to a similarly sized monitor.
