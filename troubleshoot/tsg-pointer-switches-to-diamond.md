# TSG: Pointer changes to a small diamond or star on Windows

## Overview

While using your tablet on Windows, you may see your pointer turn into a small diamond or star.

This may happen:

* momentarily when your computer first starts or you login
* after some period of time when you using your computer
* always

## Causes

The ultimate cause of seeing this pointer is that there is a problem with the tablet driver and so windows falls back to its built-in tablet drivers which are called the PNP drivers. See: [Windows PNP support](../guides/operating-systems/windows/windows-pnp-support.md).

Usually the problem is limited to the driver itself, though in theory it could be caused by the tablet having a hardware problem.

## What you can do about it

* Try restarting your computer
* Try an a newer version of your tablet driver
* Try an older version of your tablet driver
* Try using [Tablet Driver Cleanup tool](../guides/drivers/tablet-driver-cleanup-tool.md) and then reinstall the driver
* But if none of these things make a difference, contact support.
