# DIAG: Detect digitizer issues by drawing a grid

## Overview

Drawing a regular grid on your tablet can help diagnose certain kinds of problems such as&#x20;

* Digitizer failure. See [TSG: Pen skipping vertical or horizontal bands](tsg-skipping-bands.md) - where regions of the tablet simply do not detect the pen
* [Electromagnetic interference](../guides/general/electromagnetic-interference.md) where things like magnets distort straight lines    &#x20;
