# 7P: CalDigit TS4 Thunderbolt Dock

## Overview

This is one of my most-used pieces of equipment. I use it primarily to allow me switch between multiple laptops and mini-pcs on the same desk.

## Basics

* Product page: [https://www.caldigit.com/thunderbolt-station-4/](https://www.caldigit.com/thunderbolt-station-4/)

## Devices I've used with the CalDigit TS4 dock

* M3 MacBook Pro
* M3 MacMini
* M1 Mac Studio
* Surface Laptop 7
* Surface Pro 8&#x20;
* M3 iPad Pro
* Minisforum UM870

## Note on using it with Windows devices

Whenever any of my Windows computers are plugged into the dock, they do give a "too many USB hubs" warning, but everything still works.
