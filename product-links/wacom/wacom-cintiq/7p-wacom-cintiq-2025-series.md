# 7P: Wacom Cintiq 2025 series



## Overview

These tablets have just bee announced in early June. This page contains the outline of my notes for those tablets. When I have them in hand, I will add specific comments.

## Display specs

* Native resolution: 2560x1440
* Aspect ratio: 16x10

## Included Pen

Wacom Pro Pen 3 (ACP-500)

## Compatible pens

* Wacom Pro Pen 2 (KP-504E)
* Wacom Pro Pen slim (KP-301E)
* Wacom Pro Pen 3D (KP505)
* Pro Pen (KP-503E)
* Grip Pen (KP-501E)
* Classic Pen (KP-300E)
* Art Pen (KP-701E)
* Accessory Pen Black DTK-2451/DTH-2452 (KP302E)



## Touch

* Cintiq 24 touch (2025) - supports touch
* Cintiq 24 (2025) - no touch support
* Cintiq 16 (2025) - no touch support
