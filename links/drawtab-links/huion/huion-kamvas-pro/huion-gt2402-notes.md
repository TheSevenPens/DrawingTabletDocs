  * Corner: ±1mm

## Display specs

* Native resolution: 3840x2160
* Pixel density: 185 PPI
* Display tech: IPS
* Laminated: YES
* Anti-glare treatment: Etched glass
* Contrast ratio: 1000:1
* Brightness: 250 nits
* Response time  : 14ms
* Refresh rate: 60Hz

## Pens

### Included pens

* PW600
* PW600S

### Compatible pens

* And PW600 series pen will work

### Incompatible pens

* You can only use PW600 series pens with the tablet.
* Older pens like PW517, PW550, etc will not work with this tablet

## Display experience

I found the display a bit soft. (Apparently due to the etched glass). To me this was a bit softer than the Huion Kamvas Pro 19.
