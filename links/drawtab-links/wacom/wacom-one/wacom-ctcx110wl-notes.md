# Wacom One 2023 pen tablets (CTC-x110WL) notes

## Summary

<mark style="color:red;">**I do not recommend the Wacom One 2023 pen tablets because of its bad pressure handling. It produces bad strokes.**</mark>

The Wacom One 2023 pen tablets with pressure issues. See this video for details:[https://youtu.be/415ngQOHiME](https://youtu.be/415ngQOHiME)

In 2025 a firmware update did improve the pressure handling. But not enough. more here: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=\_A6hmEay-xs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_A6hmEay-xs)

{% hint style="info" %}
The Wacom One 2023 tablets are sometimes referred to as the Wacom One GEN2 tablets.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
