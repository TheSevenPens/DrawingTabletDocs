# USI

## Overview

Universal Stylus Initiative: [https://universalstylus.org/](https://universalstylus.org/)

The Universal Stylus Initiative is, or was, an organization created to define a stylus specification. Ultimately, few styluses use USI, and those that do are poor quality compared to a traditional EMR pen. I highly recommend avoiding devices that use a USI stylus.
