# 7P notes: Wacom Converter (ACK45219Z)

## Overview

The Wacom converter takes HDMI video output from your computer, a USB-data connection from you computer, and a USB power cable and combines them into a single USB-C port that you can use with the Wacom Movink 13.

The Wacom converter was intended for the Movink, but can work with non-Wacom pen displays.

## Compared to a 3-in-1 cable

The Wacom converter is essentially a $80 3-in-1 cable without the cable. To use it fully you'll need to provide 4 separate cables.

## Tablets I've tested it with

* Wacom Movink 13 - worked perfectly
* Huion Kamvas 13 GEN3 - worked perfectly
