## Driver UI > Wacom Center vs Wacom Tablet Properties

&#x20;There are two driver configuration UIs available for Wacom tablets: Wacom Center and Wacom Tablet properties.

Most features are available in both apps. But some are available only in Wacom Center.

Available in BOTH

* Tablet orientation
* Pen vs Mouse mode
* Screen area (full, portion, & specific monitors)
* Tablet area (full, portion, force proportions)
* Windows Ink&#x20;
* Tip feel (a.k.a. "the pressure curve")
* Pen Button actions

Available only in Wacom Center for Intuos Pro 2025

* ExpressKey actions
* Dial actions
