# 7P notes: Artist 16 GEN2 (CD160FH)

## Overall

Decent tablet for an entry level 16" pen display. Doe shave a noticable amount of diagonal wobble.

## Diagonal wobble

Moderate.

Diagonal wobble noticed at at most drawing speeds. Can be handled with application brush smoothing/stabilization.

## Anti-glare sparkle

LOW - maybe on the high end of low

## Noise

Totally silent

## Heat

Cool on the left side. Warm on the right especially near where the USB-C ports are.

## Ports

2 USB-C ports on the right side. Both are recessed into the tablet.
